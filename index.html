THE PROJECT

A local guide to second-hand shopping.

Christchurch has op shops spread across the city. This project brings them together in one easy-to-use interactive map so shoppers can find stores near them and explore different types of second-hand goods.

Why op shopping?

Op shopping can be a more affordable way to shop while giving pre-loved clothing, furniture, books and household items another life.

How to use the map

Search for a suburb or shop, filter by category, or click a shop in the list. Selecting a result moves the map directly to its location.

OP SHOPPING TIPS

Make the most of your hunt.

Visit different suburbs

Stock varies from store to store, so exploring different areas can uncover completely different finds.

Check back regularly

Donations change constantly. A shop that was empty last week may have something great today.

Bring an open mind

Look beyond the obvious. Books, furniture, homewares and unusual treasures can be just as rewarding as clothing.
